THE GEOMETRIC MEAN LAW
|Qij| ≈ √(|Qii|·|Qjj|) — every off-diagonal tracks the geometric mean of its diagonals.
|Q12|/|Q02| ≈ √(|Q11|/|Q00|) = 1.78 vs actual 1.80 (1.11% deviation).
Box coupling is stronger because |Q11| > |Q00| — the asymmetry is INHERITED from the diagonal ratio.

🍼 Toddler Version 🍼

Imagine three kids on a see-saw: Brace (small), Box (medium), and Torroid (big).

Torroid is the heaviest kid. When Torroid holds hands with the other two:

  • Torroid holds Box's hand TIGHTER (1.80×) because Box is bigger.
  • But if you measure how hard each kid is REACHING (compared to their own size), Brace is actually reaching further! Brace tries 1.76× harder than Box does.

There's a simple rule: how tight two kids hold hands ≈ the average of how big they each are (the geometric mean). Torroid holds Box tighter simply because Box is bigger to start with.

And the two smaller kids (Brace and Box) hold each other's hands as tight as they possibly can — they're at the maximum grip! But Torroid only uses about 1.2% of its maximum grip with either of them.

There's also a magic number: 2.23%. It keeps showing up everywhere we look, wearing different disguises. But it's always the same number — it measures how much one kid is bigger than the other.
