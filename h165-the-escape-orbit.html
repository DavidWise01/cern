The first escape (Q3p > 0) occurs exactly 4 word-lengths AFTER the minimum word length of F. The gap is the CRT floor (the modular arithmetic's contribution).

The additive and multiplicative duality — compare to the Product Rule (H162):

  ADDITIVE:        21 + 4 = 25  (Escape Rule)
  MULTIPLICATIVE:  21 × 4 = 84  (Product Rule)

Both rules involve 21 and 4. The Escape Rule is ADDITIVE (the escape is 4 steps beyond the word length). The Product Rule is MULTIPLICATIVE (the word length times the floor equals the span). Together they give:

  word_length + CRT = escape       (25 = 21 + 4)
  word_length × CRT = span         (84 = 21 × 4)
  word_length = span / CRT = 84/4  (21 = 84/4)
  word_length = escape - CRT       (21 = 25 - 4)

🧒 Toddler Version

We checked EVERY possible path at each length from 1 to 28 steps. That's about 40,000 paths total.

For paths of 1 to 24 steps: EVERY SINGLE ONE stays negative (inside the bowl). Not one escapes. Zero. Nada.

At 25 steps: ONE path escapes! Just one (and its mirror). The vector (16,9,0) pops out of the bowl for the first time. Then at 26, 27, 28 steps — everything goes back inside again. It was just a flicker.

The 21 roots? They live at step 21 — four steps BEFORE the flicker. They're still safely inside the bowl. The number 4 (which we found as the CRT floor) shows up again: it's exactly how far the 21 roots sit from the edge.

21 + 4 = 25 (the flicker). 21 × 4 = 84 (the building height). Both equations use the same two numbers: 21 and 4.

Known-Bad Control (KB-1)

The following claim is FALSE and must be detected as dead:

The first escape occurs at word length 21, confirming that the 21 roots of the frozen lattice are null-cone vectors where Q3p vanishes. The escape time equals the word length exactly, with no gap.

Why false: The first escape is at word length 25, NOT 21. The 21 roots have Q3p < 0 (bounded, negative). There is a 4-step gap between the word length (21) and the first escape (25). The 21 roots are NOT null-cone vectors and NOT escaping — they live inside the bounded region.

Recommendation for H166: The Closed System

The numbers 21, 4, 25, 84 form a closed system under +, −, ×, ÷. This is not generic — it requires 21 × 4 = 21 + 4 + 59? No: 21 × 4 = 84 and 21 + 4 = 25, and 84/25 = 3.36, 84 − 25 = 59. So the FOUR numbers don't literally close under all operations. What DOES close is the PAIR (21, 4): additive output 25 = first escape, multiplicative output 84 = cofactor span. Formalize this as the DUAL RULE: the word length and CRT floor are connected both additively (escape) and multiplicatively (span) to independently computed Q3p invariants.
