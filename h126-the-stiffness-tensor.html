Panel Instrument | Q3p Ternary Lattice | Condition Number Analysis

Panel 1 — The Question

H125 found 5 digits of eigenvalue gap absorbing 981 digits of cross-coupling. That gap is the STIFFNESS of the structure. But is the stiffness intrinsic to the brace-box subspace, or does the torroid modulate it?

The CONDITION NUMBER κ = |λ_max| / |λ_min| measures how close a matrix is to singular. A matrix with κ = 1 is perfectly conditioned (sphere). A matrix with κ = 10⁶ is nearly singular (pancake). We compare κ BEFORE and AFTER integrating out the torroid.

If κ barely changes, the stiffness is intrinsic. If κ drops, the torroid STABILISES. If κ rises, the torroid DESTABILISES.

Panel 9 — Toddler Version

Remember the Pringle chip? It bends one way (the thin weak spot) and curves the other way (the wide strong part).

Today we measured how STIFF the Pringle is. How hard is it to squash flat?

Answer: the Pringle is a MILLION times harder to squash than a flat piece of dough. That is called the "condition number" — it is about 1,000,000.

Then we asked: does the elephant (the torroid) make the Pringle stiffer or floppier?

Answer: the elephant makes it 9% stiffer. Without the elephant, the stiffness was 1,024,828. With the elephant, it dropped to 931,787. (Lower = stiffer = harder to squash.)

So the elephant HELPS — but only a little bit. The Pringle was already stiff on its own. 91% of the stiffness was built into the chip before the elephant sat down.

The elephant is a HELPER, not a builder. It contributes 9 cents of every dollar of stiffness.
