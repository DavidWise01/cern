VERDICT: 21 = 3 (COLLAPSE) × 7 (DECOUPLING)

At p=3, Q3p collapses to a perfect square (a+c)² — the box axis vanishes. At p=7, Q3p partially decouples (Q02 ≡ 0) — the brace-torroid coupling vanishes, and the nearest null-cone vector (2,1,0) is isotropic. Only p=3 and p=7 impose nontrivial constraints on the lattice, and their product is 21.

🧒 Toddler Version

The number 21 = 3 × 7. Why THOSE two numbers?

The 3 part: When you look at our matrix through "3-colored glasses," the blue direction (box) disappears completely! You can only see red (brace) and purple (torroid), and they make a perfect square: (red + purple)². The 3-glasses erase one dimension.

The 7 part: When you look through "7-colored glasses," the connection between red and purple snaps! They can't see each other anymore (Q02 = 0 in 7-land). The vector (2 red, 1 blue) is PERFECT in 7-land — it balances exactly.

Put them together (Chinese Remainder Theorem): you need to satisfy BOTH the 3-rule and the 7-rule at the same time. The smallest number that works for both is 3 × 7 = 21.

Known-Bad Control (KB-1)

The following claim is FALSE and must be detected as dead:

The frozen lattice F is exactly the sublattice of vectors where Q3p vanishes modulo 21. The minimum word length 4 in this sublattice proves that the 21-Root Theorem should actually say 4, not 21.

Why false: The mod-21 sublattice has min wl = 4, not 21. This means F is NOT the mod-21 sublattice. F has additional algebraic structure from rqt362 that makes it sparser. The 21-Root Theorem's word length 21 is correct — the mod-21 test shows that modular arithmetic alone is insufficient to define F.
