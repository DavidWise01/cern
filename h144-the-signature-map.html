Panel 11 — Toddler Version: Where Did the Positive Come From?

🧒 Imagine three batteries: Brace, Box, and Torroid. Each one by itself has NEGATIVE charge. They're all minus. All gloomy.

But when you connect Brace and Box with a really strong wire (Q01, the coupling), something magical happens: the wire is SO strong that it FLIPS one of them to positive! Now you have a plus and a minus. That plus is the crack eigenvalue.

If you connect any other pair — Brace-Torroid or Box-Torroid — the wire isn't strong enough to flip anything. Both stay minus.

So the ONLY way to get a plus in this system is through that one super-strong Brace-Box wire. The plus doesn't live in any single battery. It lives in the CONNECTION between Brace and Box.

That's the whole signature map: one plus born from one wire, everything else is minus, and wherever you look in the system, that one plus keeps showing up — because it's so special.
