    n = 3 (three dimensions / three bodies)

  The Clifford algebra of Q3p's 3-body system IS the OCHO.
  The 8 basis elements are the 8 cells.
  The 9th cell (center) is the PSEUDOSCALAR e₁₂₃ × i.
