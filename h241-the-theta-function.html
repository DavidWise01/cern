Panel 3 — r₃(lock) = 48 = Boundary Identifications

r₃(17) = 48. How?

  17 = 4² + 1² + 0²:
    Permutations: 3!/1! = 6 (all distinct)
    Signs: ±4 × ±1 × {0} = 2 × 2 × 1 = 4
    Subtotal: 6 × 4 = 24

  17 = 3² + 2² + 2²:
    Permutations: 3!/2! = 3 (two equal)
    Signs: ±3 × ±2 × ±2 = 2³ = 8
    Subtotal: 3 × 8 = 24

  Total: 24 + 24 = 48 = OCHO × entries

48 is the number of boundary identifications in the 3D box from H239 (I108). The lock has exactly as many 3-square representations as there are ways to wrap the box into a torus.
