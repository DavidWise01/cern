What this experiment establishes

Under an exactly known delay and constant drift, two consecutive delayed measurements plus the logged correction commands allow exact reconstruction of the present phase. Under an unexpected drift reversal, reconstruction errors are temporary and may worsen some outcomes. The model does not demonstrate an actual Planck-scale Rupert engine or any extractable energy from the CMB.
