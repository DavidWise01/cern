H138 — The Cubic Factorization

The structure of the characteristic polynomial's middle coefficient.

Engine: Rust BigInt (num-bigint 0.5.1) · Exact rational arithmetic · Zero floating-point

Panel 1 · The Question

H137 gave us the characteristic polynomial λ³ − c₂λ² + c₁λ − c₀ = 0 and found three eigenvalues. The middle coefficient c₁ is the sum of three 2×2 principal minors:

c₁ = M01 + M02 + M12

where:
  M01 = Q00·Q11 − Q01²    (brace ⊗ box)      = −651 digits
  M02 = Q00·Q22 − Q02²    (brace ⊗ torroid)   = +735 digits
  M12 = Q11·Q22 − Q12²    (box ⊗ torroid)     = +736 digits

M02 and M12 are 735-736 digits. M01 is only 651 digits — 85 orders of magnitude smaller. Why? What is the exact ratio M12/M02? Where does the three-scale eigenvalue hierarchy come from?

Panel 3 · The M01 Cancellation

Why is M01 only 651 digits when M02 and M12 are 735-736? The anatomy:

MinorProduct termSquare termResultDigits lost
M01Q00·Q11 = +656dQ01² = +656d−651d5 digits
M02Q00·Q22 = +735dQ02² = +733d+735d0 digits
M12Q11·Q22 = +736dQ12² = +734d+736d0 digits

M01 loses 5 digits to cancellation. Two 656-digit numbers nearly perfectly cancel, leaving a 651-digit residual. This is because Q01² ≈ Q00·Q11 — the brace-box coupling is SATURATED.

M02 and M12 lose zero digits. The product terms (735d, 736d) dominate their square terms (733d, 734d) by 2 digits. No cancellation at all — the torroid couplings are weak.

The 5-digit cancellation in M01, compounded with the 2-digit head start of M02/M12, produces the full 85-digit gap: (735−651) + rounding = 84-85 digits of scale separation.

Panel 4 · The √10 Ratio

M12 / M02 = 3.1623887404529456...

Compare: √10 = 3.1622776601683795...

                ^^^^
                matches to 4 significant figures

The box-torroid minor is approximately √10 times the brace-torroid minor. This is NOT exact — it differs at the 4th significant figure. But the near-√10 ratio has a physical origin:

M12/M02 = (Q11·Q22 − Q12²) / (Q00·Q22 − Q02²)

Since Q22 dominates (408d vs 328-329d for Q00,Q11):
  M12/M02 ≈ Q11/Q00 · (1 − Q12²/(Q11·Q22)) / (1 − Q02²/(Q00·Q22))
           ≈ |Q11|/|Q00| · (1 − 0.01200) / (1 − 0.01174)
           ≈ 3.1632 × 0.99974
           ≈ 3.1624 ✓

And |Q11|/|Q00| = 3.1632 ≈ √10 (from H133)

The minor ratio INHERITS the diagonal ratio. The √10 in the minors comes from |Q11|/|Q00| ≈ √10 — the box self-energy is √10 times the brace self-energy. The CS corrections are small (~1.2%) and nearly cancel in the ratio.

Panel 5 · The c₁ Decomposition

c₁ = M01 + M02 + M12 = +736 digits

Fraction breakdown:
  |M02| / |c₁| = 0.2402  (24.0% from brace-torroid)
  |M12| / |c₁| = 0.7598  (76.0% from box-torroid)
  |M01| / |c₁| = 10⁻⁸⁵   (≈ 0% from brace-box)

c₁ = (M02 + M12) + M01
    = +736d       + (−651d)
    = +736d  to 85-digit precision

M01 is a 10⁻⁸⁵ perturbation on c₁.

The characteristic polynomial's middle coefficient is 76% box-torroid, 24% brace-torroid, and effectively 0% brace-box. The brace-box minor is invisible in c₁.

This maps directly to the eigenvalue hierarchy: the two large eigenvalues (bond at 329d, torroid at 408d) are set by M02 and M12. The small eigenvalue (crack at 323d) comes from the residual M01.

Panel 6 · The Determinant Cofactor War

det(Q) = Q00·C00 − Q01·C01 + Q02·C02

Term 0: Q00·C00 = −1063 digits
Term 1: Q01·C01 = +1063 digits
Term 2: Q02·C02 = −1059 digits

det(Q)          = +1059 digits

Cofactor fractions of |det|:
  |Q00·C00| / |det| = 54,406   (54 thousand times det!)
  |Q01·C01| / |det| = 54,414   (54 thousand times det!)
  |Q02·C02| / |det| =  7.18    (7× det)

The determinant is the result of a WAR between three terms. Terms 0 and 1 are each 54,000 times larger than the final answer. They nearly cancel (both 1063 digits, opposite signs), and the 1059-digit residual gets a further adjustment from Term 2.

Identity confirmed: C00 = M12 (the (0,0) cofactor equals the box-torroid minor). So Term 0 = Q00 × M12.

Panel 7 · The GCD Structure

gcd(M01, M02, M12) = 36

36 = 2² × 3² = 6²

Pairwise GCDs:
  gcd(M01, M02) = small (2 digits)
  gcd(M01, M12) = small (2 digits)
  gcd(M02, M12) = small (2 digits)

All three share only the factor 36.

The GCD of all three minors is 36 = 6². This is surprisingly small — 651-to-736-digit numbers share only a 2-digit common factor. The minors are essentially coprime modulo 36.

Recall from earlier: gcd of the Q3p matrix entries themselves was 2. The minors, being products of entries minus products of entries, pick up a slightly larger shared factor: 36 = 2² × 3².

🧒 Toddler Version — The Three Handshakes

Imagine three friends in a circle: Brace, Box, and Torroid.

Brace and Box are holding hands SO TIGHT they're almost fused together. If you measured how hard they squeeze, it's 1.00002 out of 1.00000 maximum. Almost perfect grip!

When they grip that hard, something weird happens. You try to measure the SPACE between their hands, and it's really really tiny — only 651 digits long.

But Torroid holds hands with both of them really loosely — only squeezing at 0.012 out of 1.00000. So the space between Torroid's handshakes is HUGE — 735 and 736 digits!

That's like if you had three ropes. One rope is a hundred thousand billion billion billion billion billion billion billion billion billion times shorter than the other two. (That's 85 zeros!)

And the two long ropes? The box-torroid rope is √10 ≈ 3.162 times longer than the brace-torroid rope. Not exactly √10 — but close enough that you can see the pattern.

This is WHY the matrix has three different-sized eigenvalues: because one handshake is tight and two are loose!
