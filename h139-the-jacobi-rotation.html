Panel 1 — Question

Is the Q3p diagonalization literally Jacobi's method?

H122 through H138 decomposed Q3p by solving the 2×2 brace-box block, then treating the torroid as a perturbation. That's the textbook Jacobi eigenvalue algorithm — a sequence of 2×2 rotations that zero off-diagonal elements. But we never named it. This instrument computes the explicit Jacobi rotation parameters and measures what one rotation actually does.

Two meanings of "Jacobi": (1) Jacobi coordinates — the three-body transform separating a tight pair from the third body. (2) Jacobi's eigenvalue method — iterative 2×2 rotations to diagonalize a symmetric matrix. Both apply simultaneously.

Key: tan(2θ) > 1 → 2θ > 45° → θ > 22.5°. With θ ≈ 29.35° (from H122), the rotation is SUBSTANTIAL — not a small perturbation. The gcd = 1 means the angle fraction cannot be simplified. The rational cannot even be reduced.

Discriminant (p²+q²): 657 digits. NOT a perfect square → the Jacobi angle θ is IRRATIONAL (algebraic degree 2). It's a quadratic irrational, like √2. This is the eigenvalue gap of the 2×2 brace-box block, and it cannot be expressed as a rational angle.

The paradox:

The first Jacobi rotation "zeroes" Q'[0][1] = 0 — but that removes only 656 digits of norm from a 734-digit total. That's 10⁻⁷⁸ of the total off-diagonal energy. The rotation doesn't shrink the off-diagonal — it separates it into two independent scales.

The 78-digit invisibility = 734 − 656 = 78. The 85-digit gap from H138 (between M01 and M02/M12) manifests here as a 78-digit norm gap. The first Jacobi rotation is cosmetically invisible but structurally decisive.

Panel 11 — Toddler Version: The Dance

🧒 Imagine three people at a dance party: Brace, Box, and Torroid.

Brace and Box are DANCE PARTNERS — they're spinning together so close they're practically one person. They spin at a special angle: 29.35 degrees. That's how tilted they are.

Torroid is standing a LONG WAY across the room. Torroid wants to dance too, and waves at both of them. Torroid waves mostly at the PAIR (99.998%) and barely at each one separately (0.002%).

Now here's the magic trick: when Brace and Box STOP spinning and stand still (that's what the Jacobi rotation does — it lines them up so they're not tangled), Torroid's wave doesn't get smaller. It's the SAME wave. But now we can see that almost ALL the wave is going to one of them (Bond) and almost NONE to the other (Crack).

The dance didn't make Torroid quieter. It made us able to SEE who Torroid was really waving at.

That's what "mode isolation" means: untangling who is talking to whom, without changing how loud they're talking.
