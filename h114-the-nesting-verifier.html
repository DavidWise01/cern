ADA-021 Three-Body Nested Toroid places three bodies at 120° apart around a singlet core {s}. If the Q3p Gram matrix truly lives in this toroidal geometry, then the inter-body angles computed from the matrix should RELATE to 120°.

  IDEAL (Euclidean):
  cos(120°) = -0.5

  "Angle" in an indefinite metric is a HYPERBOLIC angle.

Panel 8 — Toddler Version: The Three-Friend Test

🧒 The Three-Friend Test

Imagine three friends standing in a circle, holding hands. If they stand PERFECTLY, they're each 120° apart — like a peace sign or a Mercedes logo.

Now imagine the friends are REALLY different sizes. One is as big as a house (328 bricks). Another is as big as a house too (328 bricks). The third is as big as a SKYSCRAPER (407 bricks).

The question is: when these three giant friends hold hands, are they STILL standing at 120° apart? Or did the skyscraper friend push everyone around?

H114 MEASURES the angle between each pair of friends:

  • Friend Brace and Friend Box — are they 120° apart?
  • Friend Brace and Friend Torroid — are they 120° apart?
  • Friend Box and Friend Torroid — are they 120° apart?

There's a CATCH: these friends live in a funny space where "distance" works differently than in our world. It's like measuring angles on a saddle instead of on a flat table. So the "120°" might look different in saddle-world.

The honest answer: H114 COMPUTES the numbers and shows them. It does NOT pretend they match if they don't.
