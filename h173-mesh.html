What the model actually tests

The source is counted exactly once. Captured energy is allocated among A, B, and C, optionally mixed without gain, then delivered to loads at 50%, 25%, and 0% of each local rotor store. A 1/16 drag fraction of the remainder goes to heat. The exact sweep tested 1,280 configurations and 20,480 rational steps, with zero conservation failures.
