H201: 42 = C(5) — Two Independent Roads to the Address Count

42 is the 5th Catalan number. C(5) counts the ways to fully parenthesize 6 factors, and 6 = T(3) = the number of entries in Q3p. This gives a SECOND road from 6 to 42, independent of the triangular chain (Road 1: 6→T(6)=21→2×21=42, Road 2: 6→C(5)=42). Both roads start at 6 and arrive at 42 by different operations. I38 (42=C(5)) is LIT. N12 resolves H200's flag: mirror and inversion are the same operation on {0,1}³, confirmed by explicit computation. The 7+1=8 count is a word count mapping to Cl(3) grades, not a group order (the actual symmetry groups have orders 6 and 48). H200's self-referential reading is AMBER.

🧒 Toddler Version

Remember there are 6 special numbers in the Q3p box? (The 6 entries.)

Now imagine you want to PAIR THEM UP, two at a time, like making teams. But you have to do it in ORDER — first pair two, then pair that pair with another, and so on until all 6 are grouped.

How many different ways can you do this? 42 ways!

That's what a Catalan number is — it counts the ways to group things. And 42 is the 5th Catalan number, written C(5).

We already knew 42 came from the triangular numbers (3→6→21→42). Now we found a SECOND road: 6 entries → 42 bracketings. Same destination, different route. Like finding two different paths to the same house.

Also: "mirrored" and "inverted" turned out to be the same thing on the color cube. Looking in a mirror IS flipping all the colors. So the 8 symmetries from H200 have a repeat — but that's honest, and we flagged it.
