H069 — Three-Witness Receipt Anchoring

ASCII-only experiment. Every attestation must bind to the same custodian-signed receipt; three disjoint source roots are required.

                   |

  A + A-MIRROR + B = ONLY TWO ROOTS
  A + B + D[ROOTS R1,R4] = TWO ROOTS

  EQUIVOCATION FROM ONE WITNESS = FREEZE
  REPLAYED IDENTICAL ATTESTATION = COUNT ONCE
