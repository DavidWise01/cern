Imagine a swimming pool. When you look at the surface from far away, it looks smooth. But if you had magic goggles that could zoom in a BILLION BILLION BILLION BILLION times (that's 1035), you might see the surface of space itself getting all bubbly and foamy — that's what scientists call Planck foam.
