Known-Bad Control (KB-1)

The following claim is FALSE and must be detected as dead:

The Clifford algebra Cl(3) and the QCD gauge group SU(3) are isomorphic algebras with identical mathematical structure. Their shared dimension of 8 proves they are the same object, and no distinction is needed.
