The 2×2 brace-box block has eigenvalue ratio |λ_small|/|λ_large| ≈ 3.09×10−6 (H134). The small eigenvalue carries 323 digits of exact information.

What DIRECTION does the small eigenvector point? It is the one degree of freedom separating brace from box — the crack in the bond.

How does that direction relate to the torroid coupling direction from H132 (φ = 60.92°)?

If the torroid couples along the unlocked direction, the two-scale structure has a physical explanation: the torroid exploits the one weakness in the bond.

Panel 3 — H132 Correction: The Sign Error

CORRECTION TO H132

H132 computed the torroid coupling direction as φ = atan(|Q12|/|Q02|) = 60.92° using UNSIGNED magnitudes.

The actual torroid coupling vector [Q02, Q12] = [−367d, +367d] points into the SECOND quadrant.

Signed direction: atan2(+Q12, Q02) = atan2(+, −) ≈ 119.08°, not 60.92°.

IMPROVEMENT RECOMMENDATION → H136: The Antiparallel Analysis

The torroid is antiparallel to the bond eigenvector (179.73°, not 0°). Does this mean the torroid OPPOSES the bond or REINFORCES it? The sign of the dot product is NEGATIVE. For a quadratic form Q with eigenvector v and eigenvalue λ, the coupling c = torroid · v adds a term c²/λ to the effective eigenvalue. With λ < 0 and c² > 0, the correction c²/λ is NEGATIVE: the torroid makes the bond MORE negative — it DEEPENS the bond, it does not oppose it. Compute the exact perturbation theory: how much does the torroid shift each eigenvalue? What is the corrected eigenvalue ratio?
