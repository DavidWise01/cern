Three superconducting toroids. Twenty-four coils.

Engineering-based topology of the ATLAS detector at LHC Point 1. The four LHC detectors around the ring are not four toroids.

ATLAS toroidal field

Count

3 toroid magnet assemblies

8 + 8 + 8 = 24 superconducting coils

1 separate central solenoid

1.58 GJ design stored energy, toroids combined

45° rotation between neighboring coils in one toroid

22.5° barrel/end-cap coil angular offset

Nominal construction values; not live operating measurements.

One LHC, four detectors

Magnet roles

ATLAS · Point 1: barrel + 2 end-cap toroids and a central solenoid.

ALICE · Point 2: resistive solenoid and dipole.

CMS · Point 5: superconducting solenoid.

LHCb · Point 8: resistive dipole.

A toroid is a magnet assembly that creates a toroidal field. The central solenoid does not count toward the 24 toroid coils.
