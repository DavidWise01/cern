Homeostasis

Each module has three normalized coordinates bounded by h=(31/36,1,11/18). Its total exchange and disturbance budgets are each 1/36. Neighbors are averaged, not summed. The product box remains invariant for every finite module count. 24,000 exact module updates, no violations.

Helium is a conceptual muse, not an atomic simulation: actual helium has two electrons and a nucleus containing two protons and, for helium-4, two neutrons. Noble gas chemistry requires quantum electronic structure.
