H136: The Antiparallel Analysis

Exact perturbation theory: how the torroid shifts each eigenvalue of the 2×2 brace-box block

Engine: Rust + num-bigint | Instrument class: Perturbation correction

Panel 1 — The Question

H135 showed the torroid is antiparallel to the bond eigenvector (179.73°), coupling 99.998% along bond.

The torroid pushes along the locked direction, not the crack.

So what does that push DO to the eigenvalues?

Second-order perturbation theory: Δλ_i = c_i² / (|v_i|² × (λ_i − Q22))

where c_i = torroid·v_i is the coupling projection onto eigenvector i.

Hypothesis entering this instrument: with λ_bond < 0, the correction should be negative, deepening the bond.

⚡ LEVEL REPULSION ⚡

The torroid WEAKENS the bond, not deepens it.

Q22 (−408d) is a deeper well than λ_bond (−329d).

λ_bond − Q22 = (−329d) − (−408d) = POSITIVE

c_bond² / POSITIVE = POSITIVE correction → bond gets LESS negative

The initial hypothesis forgot that the denominator is (λ − Q22), not just λ.

When the perturbing level (Q22) lies BELOW the eigenvalue, repulsion pushes UP.

Panel 4 — Schur Complement (Exact, All Orders)

The Schur complement integrates out the torroid EXACTLY.
No perturbation approximation — this is the full answer.

Schur(2×2 | torroid) = M_bb − col × Q22⁻¹ × row

where:
  M_bb = [[Q00, Q01], [Q01, Q11]]  (the 2×2 brace-box block)
  col  = [Q02, Q12]^T              (torroid coupling column)
  row  = [Q02, Q12]                (torroid coupling row)
  Q22  = torroid self-energy

S[i][j] = Q[i][j] − Q[i][2]×Q[j][2] / Q22

Results (numerator × Q22):
  S00_num = + sign, 735d
  S01_num = − sign, 735d
  S11_num = + sign, 736d

Schur eigenvalue ratio: |λ_S_bond| / |λ_S_crack| = 294,571
  → |λ_S_crack| / |λ_S_bond| = 1/294,571 = 3.39×10⁻⁶

  MATCHES perturbation theory corrected ratio: 3.39×10⁻⁶ ✓

  Schur complement (exact) confirms second-order perturbation.
  Higher orders are present but negligible at this scale.

Panel 6 — Physical Interpretation

THE LEVEL REPULSION PICTURE:

  Energy
    ↑
    |  λ_crack (+323d) ──→ pushed UP (+8.6%)  ──→ more positive
    |
    |  ───── zero ─────
    |
    |  λ_bond  (−329d) ──→ pushed UP (+1.2%)  ──→ less negative
    |
    |
    |
    |  Q22     (−408d) ──→ the DEEPEST level   ──→ anchored
    ↓

  RULE: when two levels are coupled, they REPEL each other.
  The closer they are in energy, the stronger the repulsion.

  Q22 is below BOTH eigenvalues.
  Therefore BOTH eigenvalues are pushed UP (away from Q22).

  WHY THE HYPOTHESIS FAILED:
  ─────────────────────────
  The hypothesis said: Δλ = c²/λ, with λ < 0 → negative correction.
  But the ACTUAL formula is: Δλ = c²/(λ − Q22).
  Since Q22 < λ_bond < 0, the denominator (λ_bond − Q22) is POSITIVE.
  So c² / POSITIVE = POSITIVE. The bond moves UP, not DOWN.

  The error was treating Q22 as if it were at zero.
  Q22 is not at zero. Q22 is the deepest thing in the matrix.

  CONSEQUENCE:
  ────────────
  The torroid makes the brace-box bond WEAKER, not stronger.
  By 1.2% (relative) — a small but nonzero effect.
  The crack gets 8.6% wider (relative to its small eigenvalue).
  The eigenvalue ratio widens from 3.09×10⁻⁶ to 3.39×10⁻⁶.
  The two-scale structure (10⁻⁶ ratio) PERSISTS through the correction.

Panel 8 — Toddler Version

🏊 THE SWIMMING POOL STORY 🏊

Imagine three diving boards at a swimming pool:

  HIGH BOARD: the crack — a little bit above the water (+323)
  LOW BOARD:  the bond  — below the water (−329)
  DEEP END:   the torroid — WAY down at the bottom (−408)

We thought: when the deep end pushes on the low board,
it would push it DOWN (deeper underwater).

But that's not how it works!

When two things are connected, they PUSH APART.
Like two magnets with the same pole facing each other.

The deep end (−408) pushes the low board (−329) UP.
The deep end (−408) pushes the high board (+323) UP.

EVERYTHING moves AWAY from the deepest thing.

So the torroid doesn't make the bond stronger.
It makes the bond a TINY BIT weaker.
The low board moves up a little (1.2%).
The high board moves up a little more (8.6%).

The gap between the boards gets WIDER.
Before: the boards were 3.09 millionths apart (ratio).
After: they're 3.39 millionths apart.

The two-arm-in-arm friends from H135?
The wind from behind (the torroid) is loosening
their grip SLIGHTLY — not tightening it.
