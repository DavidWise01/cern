Chapter XXXII — The Cost of Turning

Jane tracked the fuel. Patricia opened the torque gate. Toph examined the opposite rotation of the housing. Every pulse moved energy, but none appeared from nowhere. When the flywheel gave work to the load, its motion diminished. When friction slowed the rotor, heat appeared instead. The remaining question was not whether it could spin but how effectively it could deliver the stored motion.
