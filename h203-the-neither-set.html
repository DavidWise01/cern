🧒 Toddler Version

Remember our 15 special numbers split into three teams?

Team Catalan (2 players): 2 and 42. They count ways to group things.

Team Triangular (5 players): 3, 6, 10, 21, 36. They count pairs.

That leaves 8 numbers on NO team: 4, 8, 17, 18, 25, 84, 289, 336.

But they're not random! They're all made from just TWO numbers — 42 and 8 — using simple math: add them, subtract them, multiply them, halve them, square them.

Like having two Lego pieces and building 8 different things by snapping them together different ways. Half of 8 is 4. 42 minus 8 divided by 2 is 17. 42 times 8 is 336. Every one of the 8 "leftover" numbers comes from the same two building blocks.

And there are exactly 8 of them — same as OCHO, one of the building blocks!

Known-Bad Control (KB-1)

The following claim is FALSE and must be detected as dead:

The "neither" set contains 6 members (not 8), and three of them — 17, 25, and 289 — cannot be expressed as combinations of G=42 and OCHO=8 at all, requiring a third independent generator. The roster partitions into four families, not three, with 15 = 3+4+6+2.

The set has exactly 8 members. ALL eight are expressible from G and OCHO: 17=(42−8)/2, 25=(42+8)/2, 289=17²=((42−8)/2)². No third generator needed. Three families, 15=2+5+8.
