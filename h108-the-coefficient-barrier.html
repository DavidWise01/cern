Panel 4 — The Unseen Solution

By the Minkowski bound for ternary forms:

||v||² ≤ (4/3) · |det(Q3_prim)|1/3 ≈ 10353

So each coordinate of the solution vector v = (v0, v1, v2) has at most ~176 digits (~585 bits).

The search space for three coordinates of ~176 digits each is ~10529 vectors. For comparison, the number of atoms in the observable universe is ~1080.

The solution IS there. Eichler-Kneser guarantees it. All five local conditions are satisfied (H107). But the gap between existence and construction is:

EXISTENCE (theoretical)  ↔  10529 search space  ↔  10100 factoring ops  ↔  CONSTRUCTION (computational)
