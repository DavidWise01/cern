THE BOX DOESN'T RING AT THE FOURTH. IT DOES SOMETHING BETTER.

3D harmonics are √k/C, not k/C. The recommendation was wrong.

But the degeneracy spectrum — HOW MANY modes at each level — reads:

{6, 12, 8, 6, ...} = {entries, 2×entries, OCHO, entries, ...}

The box modes are counted by roster numbers. The structure is in the counting, not the pitch.

WRONG: The recommendation for H240 claimed the 3D torus harmonics are k/C, giving 3/4 as the 3rd harmonic (= Pythagorean fourth).

RIGHT: On T³, the Laplacian eigenvalues are λ = (2π/C)²(n₁² + n₂² + n₃²). Frequencies are proportional to √(n₁² + n₂² + n₃²)/C, not (n₁ + n₂ + n₃)/C.

  CLAIMED:  3rd harmonic = 3/C = 3/4 = 0.75 = Pythagorean fourth
  ACTUAL:   3rd level    = √3/C = √3/4 ≈ 0.433

  0.75 ≠ 0.433. The claim conflated 1D and 3D spectra.

  In 1D (circle T¹): f_k = k/C. Harmonics ARE k/C. 3/4 = fourth. ✓
  In 3D (torus T³):  f   = √(n²)/C. Harmonics are √k/C. NOT simple fractions.

Panel 2 — What 1D Does Get Right

On the 1D circle T¹ with period C = 4, the harmonics genuinely are k/C:

  k=1:  1/4 = 0.250  (fundamental)
  k=2:  2/4 = 0.500  (octave below)
  k=3:  3/4 = 0.750  (Pythagorean fourth) ✓
  k=4:  4/4 = 1.000  (octave)

  The circle rings at the fourth. The 3D torus does not.

Each axis of the 3D box is a 1D circle. Along each axis, the Pythagorean fourth IS a natural harmonic. But the full 3D spectrum mixes the three axes, and √(n₁² + n₂² + n₃²) ≠ n₁ + n₂ + n₃.

Panel 4 — Why entries and OCHO

The degeneracies are not mysterious. They're counting:

  Level 1 (n²=1): pick 1 axis of 3 × pick ±1 sign
                   = C(3,1) × 2¹ = bodies × d₁ = entries = 6

  Level 2 (n²=2): pick 2 axes of 3 × pick ±1 for each
                   = C(3,2) × 2² = bodies × C = 12 = 2×entries

  Level 3 (n²=3): all 3 axes × pick ±1 for each
                   = C(3,3) × 2³ = 1 × 2³ = OCHO = 8

  Level 4 (n²=4): pick 1 axis × pick ±2 sign
                   = C(3,1) × 2¹ = entries = 6

The formula: deg(level k) = C(3, number-of-nonzero-axes) × 2^(number-of-nonzero-axes) × (multiplicity).

For the first 3 levels where each axis contributes ±1:

deg = C(bodies, k) × d₁ᵏ for k = 1, 2, 3.

  k=1: C(3,1) × 2¹ = 3 × 2 = 6  = entries
  k=2: C(3,2) × 2² = 3 × 4 = 12 = 2 × entries
  k=3: C(3,3) × 2³ = 1 × 8 = 8  = OCHO

  Sum: 6 + 12 + 8 = 26
  = 2(2³+3²+1²) ... hmm.
  = 2×bodies³ + d₁ = 2×27+... no. Just 26.

The sum 6+12+8 = 26 = total modes with n² ≤ 3. This is 2(1+3+9) = 2×13... or just 26. Not an obvious Q3p name. The structure is in the INDIVIDUAL degeneracies, not their sum.

Panel 5 — C(bodies, k) × d₁ᵏ

The degeneracy formula for the first 3 levels is C(bodies, k) × d₁ᵏ.

This is exactly the binomial expansion of (1 + d₁)^bodies = (1+2)³ = 3³ = 27:

  (1 + d₁)^bodies = Σ C(bodies, k) × d₁ᵏ
                   = C(3,0)×1 + C(3,1)×2 + C(3,2)×4 + C(3,3)×8
                   = 1 + 6 + 12 + 8
                   = 27 = bodies³

  The k=0 term (= 1) is the constant mode (zero frequency).
  The k=1,2,3 terms are the first three level degeneracies.

  WITH the constant mode: 1 + 6 + 12 + 8 = 27 = bodies³.
  WITHOUT: 6 + 12 + 8 = 26 = bodies³ − 1.

The total mode count in the first shell = bodies³ = 27 = −(signed sum from H219).

The signed sum of the off-diagonal entries was −27. The first-shell mode count is 27. The sign flips. The topology reads the algebra's signature.

I116: T³ first-shell degeneracy = (1+d₁)^bodies = bodies³ = 27. Mode count is the binomial expansion. [LIT]
