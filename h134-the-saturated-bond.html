Panel 1 — The Question

H133 found Q01²/(Q00·Q11) = 1.000017 — the Cauchy-Schwarz bound for the brace-box pair is saturated to 5 decimal places. For positive-definite matrices, saturation means the two vectors are parallel (rank 1). But Q00 and Q11 are both NEGATIVE (indefinite form). What does the residual R = Q01² − Q00·Q11 look like exactly?

🍼 Toddler Version 🍼

Imagine Brace and Box are holding hands SO TIGHTLY they almost look like one person. If you squint, you'd think they're glued together — they match to 99.998%.

But they're NOT actually glued. There's a teeny tiny gap — like a hair's width. That gap has 651 digits (the numbers that describe it are HUGE, just 100,000× smaller than the bond itself).

Now Torroid stands a little ways apart from them. It holds one hand with Brace and one with Box, but its grip is loose — about 1.2% of maximum squeeze. And the way it reaches toward them DOESN'T follow the same rule that Brace and Box use with each other.

Brace and Box are like best friends walking arm-in-arm. Torroid is like a third kid walking behind them, holding the edge of each one's sleeve.

There are two different levels of togetherness: the best-friend bond (super tight, 0.002% gap) and the sleeve-hold (1.2% grip). They're 1000× different in strength.
