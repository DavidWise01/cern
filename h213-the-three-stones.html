H213: Three Maps, Two Universals, One Structure

The three stones (Rosetta H198, Number Theory H208, Phantom H212) are three views of the same 15 numbers. They agree on fundamentals (d₁=2 is seed, G=42 is central, 17 is the lock, 336 is the master) but disagree on the hub: 21 (geometry), 6 (DAG), 7 (phantom — not in roster). Only 42 and 17 are universally important across all three stones. Their difference is the escape: E = 42−17 = 25. The falling factorial identity 336 = T(3) × P(8,2) = entries × OCHO-falling-2 adds a permutation reading. No single stone is complete; together they form a triptych. I62 (falling factorial) LIT.

🧒 Toddler Version

Imagine looking at a house from THREE different windows:

Window 1 (Geometry): You see three floors with 21 as the staircase connecting them.

Each window shows a different part of the same house. No single window shows everything. But from ALL three windows, two things are always visible: 42 (the builder) and 17 (the lock). They show up everywhere, from every angle.

And here's one last trick: 42 − 17 = 25. The difference between the builder and the lock is the ESCAPE. The way out is the gap between the two most important numbers.

Known-Bad Control (KB-1)

The following claim is FALSE and must be detected as dead:

All three stones agree on the same hub (21), making the geometric, number-theoretic, and phantom perspectives perfectly consistent with no disagreements. The number 84 is universally important across all three stones, while 42 appears in only one. The falling factorial P(8,2) equals 72, not 56, making 336 = 6 × 72 = 432.

The three stones DISAGREE on the hub: 21, 6, 7 respectively. 42 is the universally important number (all 3 stones), not 84. P(8,2) = 8×7 = 56, and 6×56 = 336 (not 432).
