The H161 experiment

Eleven rank-three fractures, five signed observation-lineage patterns and four adversarial evidence variants per fracture: 99 tests total. The 165 signatures across the five lineage patterns verified correctly, but 44 copied-information configurations were rejected for shared observation ancestry. Eleven independent-information cases were accepted in the synthetic test.
