Recommendation

H107: The Ternary Oracle. Root 19 existence reduces to: does the ternary quadratic form Q3 (3×3, 644-bit LLL-reduced, signature (1+,2−), det 3515 bits) represent −2 over the integers? This is the Hasse-Minkowski question for a specific ternary form. The local conditions are satisfied (no p-adic obstruction found). What remains is the global-to-local bridge: does Hasse-Minkowski hold for this form? For ternary forms over Z, it does NOT hold unconditionally—counterexamples exist. The next step is a Smith normal form + genus analysis of Q3: compute the genus of the form, determine whether −2 is represented by the genus, then apply Kneser’s neighbor method or Siegel’s mass formula to decide global representability. Source: the Q3 matrix from RQT-388. If the genus contains −2 but no form in the genus represents it: minimum = 23 and the frozen chain terminates at 18.
