🧒 Toddler Version

The color cube has 8 colors. We made a BIG multiplication chart: every color times every other color. That's 8 × 8 = 64 squares in the chart.

8 squares are on the diagonal (a color times itself). The other 56 squares are where you multiply two DIFFERENT colors.

Of those 56, 36 give a positive answer and 20 give a negative answer. And 36 is one of our special Q3p numbers! It's T(8) — the same number that counts the gcd minors. The Clifford algebra KNOWS about Q3p's minors.

Also: half the self-multiplications give +1 (the first 4 colors) and half give −1 (the last 4 colors). The number 4 — the escape gap — is the BOUNDARY between the plus world and the minus world.
