Panel 9 — Toddler Version

Imagine you have a big donut, a stick, and a box.

The donut is SO BIG it's basically the whole toy (H128). And it's made of one unbreakable piece (H129).

Today we asked: if we TAKE AWAY the donut, what's left?

Answer: the same stick and box, just 1.2% smaller.

The donut didn't twist them. Didn't flip them. Didn't squish one more than the other. It just made everything a TINY bit smaller — equally, all around.

Like shrinking a photo by 1% — everything gets smaller but the picture looks the same. That's called conformal — "same shape, different size."

And something spooky: the stick and box are NOT the same strength. One is a MILLION times stronger than the other. The donut didn't fix that. It just made both of them 1.2% quieter. The imbalance stayed.

The donut is a volume knob, not an equaliser.
