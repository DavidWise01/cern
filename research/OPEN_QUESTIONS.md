# Open provenance questions

## Q1: Ferranti Mercury original magnetic media (1958–1966)
CERN confirms core memory, magnetic drums and two Ampex tape units added in 1962. **No verified original CERN tape image or REAP tape reel has been located.** Determine physical holding institution, accession ID, state of media, format and transfer history.
Sources: https://drupal-timeline.web.cern.ch/cern-installs-its-first-electronic-computer-ferranti-mercury ; https://archives.cern/

## Q2: REAP source and IBM ports
Locate REAP original source listing and an IBM 709/7090 revision. Compare input fields, BCD record layout, output, algorithm and version/port authors. Scientific function similarity is not source-line inheritance.
Primary records: https://cds.cern.ch/record/223864/files/p1.pdf?version=1 ; https://cds.cern.ch/record/297896/files/CERN-TC-PHYSICS-63-11.pdf

## Q3: Technology vs executable continuity
Separate physical hardware succession, copied binary, translated source, compatible instruction sets, data migration, and institutional function continuity. Root links without accession or source lineage remain hypotheses.

## Q4: Current public completeness
Enumerate CERN Open Data records with pagination, date, licensing, and changes. Track CERN GitLab/GitHub repos separately, deduplicate shared roots; do not claim this catalogue covers every publicly accessible CERN item.

## Q5: Funding, contractors, and ownership
Separate taxpayer funding, purchased equipment, public-owned assets, software licenses, vendor service agreements, and later private returns. Track original contracts and ownership evidence; historical coincidence alone is not evidence of asset transfer.

## First physical archive leads
CERN Archives (https://archives.cern/) and the Academy of Mining and Metallurgy in Kraków that received Mercury in 1966. Also use the Internet Archive Ferranti July 1961 publication https://archive.org/details/bitsavers_ferrantiFeatureJul61_763465 for architecture context, not as a claimed magnetic tape dump.
