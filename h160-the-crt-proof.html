Panel 1 — What We Asked

H159 showed 21 = 3 × 7 where p=3 is the collapse prime (box vanishes) and p=7 is the decoupling prime (Q02 ≡ 0). The Chinese Remainder Theorem combines these local constraints into a mod-21 sublattice. Does the CRT sublattice have minimum word length 21?

Panel 3 — The Vector (3, −1, 0)

Known-Bad Control (KB-1)

The following claim is FALSE and must be detected as dead:

The CRT combination of mod-3 and mod-7 constraints produces a sublattice whose minimum word length is exactly 21, completing the proof that 21 = 3 × 7 follows from the local structure alone without rqt362.

Why false: CRT(3,7) gives min wl = 4 at (3,−1,0), not 21. The local structure at primes 3 and 7 is NECESSARY but NOT SUFFICIENT. The gap from 4 to 21 requires deeper p-adic or algebraic constraints from rqt362. The proof remains incomplete.
