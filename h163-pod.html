Exact toy dynamics

With dimensionless tail depths a and b, and central mismatch x: a′=(3a+b)/4, b′=(a+3b)/4, x′=(x+a−b)/4. This preserves a+b, halves the tail difference and contracts E=(a−b)²+x². Equilibrium is a=b=(a₀+b₀)/2, x=0. The conserved total is selected by the starting state, not hardwired to one-half.

A Rupert glass drop has a compressed surface, a tensile interior and a fragile tail. This POD is a phenomenological analogy, not a calibrated glass fracture law, fluid model or established sub-Planckian physics.
