Known-Bad Control (KB-1)

The following claim is FALSE and must be detected as dead:

The Smith Normal Form directly gives d₁ = 21 as the first invariant factor, proving that 21 is a fundamental divisibility property of Q3p's entries. The minimum word length is simply the GCD of the matrix entries.

Why false: d₁ = gcd(entries) = 2, not 21. The minimum word length 21 does NOT appear in the Smith form. It appears as HALF the cofactor gap (42/2), which is a scale relationship in the adjugate, not a divisibility property of the entries. The connection is observed, not proved.
