🧒 Toddler Version

Imagine two families of special numbers, like two teams:

Team Catalan (C): 1, 1, 2, 5, 14, 42, 132, 429...

Team Triangular (T): 1, 3, 6, 10, 15, 21, 28, 36...

The only number on BOTH teams is 1. That's it! After 1, the two teams never share a player again.

Now look at our 15 special Q3p numbers: some play for Team Catalan (2 and 42), some for Team Triangular (3, 6, 10, 21, 36), and the rest play for neither team. But NOBODY plays for both teams at the same time.

The two paths to 42 we found yesterday? They really are different paths. They use different kinds of numbers. Like two roads that cross the same river at different bridges.
