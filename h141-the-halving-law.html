Does the gap cascade continue halving? 85 → 42 → 21?

H138 found an 84-digit gap in the minor hierarchy. H140 found that the Schur complement halved it to 42 digits. The prediction: a second Schur elimination should halve it again to ~21 digits. This instrument tests that prediction by completing the full Schur descent from 3×3 to 2×2 to 1×1, and by trying ALL THREE pivots.

Panel 4 — Why The Halving Breaks

The halving prediction assumed each Schur step would independently halve the gap. But the second step encounters a different kind of saturation:

Level 1:  S_scaled = [[M01(−651d), X(−693d)], [X(−693d), M02(+735d)]]

Level 2:  σ = M02 − X²/M01
            = 735d − (1385d / 651d)
            = 735d − 734d
            = ~1d residual

The correction X²/M01 is 734 digits — only ONE digit smaller than M02.
This IS the Cauchy-Schwarz saturation at the Schur level:

  CS(Schur) = X²/(|M01|·M02) = 0.0865

The CS fraction says X² is 8.65% of |M01|·M02. But the CORRECTION
X²/M01 ≈ M02 means X² ≈ M01·M02. The saturation reappears as a
near-equality between the correction and the target.

Panel 10 — Toddler Version: The Peacemaker

🧒 Remember Brace and Box are best friends who squeeze SO tight they're practically one person? And Torroid is the one standing across the room?

Last time we tried removing Brace from the room (the Schur complement). When Brace left, the squeeze between Box and Torroid was gentle — a 42-digit gap, nice and tidy.

Now we tried removing Torroid instead. And guess what? WITHOUT Torroid in the room, Brace and Box go right back to squeezing too tight! The gap collapses to just 1 digit. The pathology comes right back.

Torroid is the PEACEMAKER. When Torroid is there, Brace and Box can relax because Torroid gives them something else to pay attention to. Take the peacemaker away, and the two go right back to their old ways.

That's why the halving law works once but not twice. The first step removes the troublemaker (brace) — gap halves. The second step removes the peacemaker (torroid's effect through the Schur complement) — gap collapses.
