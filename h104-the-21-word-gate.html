Panel 5 — The Product Gate

The final question. Everything else is preparation.

  Given 21 frozen roots r₁, r₂, ..., r₂₁ in K = h⊥ ∩ M:

      R₁ · R₂ · ... · R₂₁  =?=  X

  where Rⱼ = I + rⱼ(rⱼᵀG) is the reflection through rⱼ.

  WHAT WE KNOW:
    ✓  Such a product is an isometry of G           (each Rⱼ is)
    ✓  Such a product fixes h                       (each Rⱼ does)
    ✓  det = (-1)²¹ = -1 = det(X)                  (parity matches)
    ✓  The 21 roots span an index-64 sublattice     (required, and B does)
    ✓  The Wall form is integral upper unitriangular (required, and H is)

  WHAT REMAINS:
    ◐  Root 18 must be certified (candidate exists)
    ○  Roots 19-20 must be found (kernel ladder)
    ○  Root 21 is forced (unique ±1)
    ○  PRODUCT MUST EQUAL X (the actual gate)

  IF THE PRODUCT = X:
    → Minimum word length is 21 (not 23)
    → The frozen lattice construction is COMPLETE
    → X ∈ W₋₂(K) with a minimal-length word

  IF THE PRODUCT ≠ X:
    → Either a different ordering works
    → Or 23 is genuinely minimal
    → The Wall-integral factorization program fails

OPEN This gate will not close until all 21 roots exist and the product is computed.

The Toddler Version — The Magic Spell

Imagine you have a magic mirror. It does something special — it flips things around in a funny way. You want to figure out the SECRET SPELL that makes the mirror do its trick.

You know the spell is made of MAGIC WORDS. Each word is a little flip. When you say all the words together, they make the big mirror flip.

Some people found a spell with 25 words. Then someone made it shorter — 23 words! But we think there might be an even shorter spell with just 21 words.

We're looking for those 21 words. We already found 17 of them! We know what word 18 probably is. Words 19 and 20 are hiding in a smaller and smaller space. And word 21? Once we find 19 and 20, word 21 is the ONLY word left — it's forced!

The BIG question: when we say all 21 words together... do they make the SAME mirror flip? That's the gate we need to open.
