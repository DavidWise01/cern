Panel 9 — Toddler Version

Yesterday we said the donut shrinks EVERYTHING the same — like zooming out a photo.

Today we looked REALLY carefully. With a magnifying glass.

And guess what? The donut shrinks the box a TINY bit more than the stick.

Stick shrinks by 1.174%. Box shrinks by 1.200%. That's a difference of 0.026%.

Is that just because our calculator rounded? NO! We checked with EXACT multiplication — no rounding at all — and the difference is REAL.

It's like the donut is SLIGHTLY tilted. It sits a little closer to the box than the stick.

And the coolest part: the shrinking goes in EVEN STEPS. Stick → cross → box. Each step is the same size. Like stairs.

The donut isn't a perfect zoom-out. It's a zoom-out with a tiny tilt.

Like when you photocopy something but the paper is a LITTLE BIT crooked. Everything gets smaller, AND it leans a tiny bit to one side.
