Panel 6 — The Plateau Theorem

  THE PLATEAU:

  3-adic:  1 → 1 → 1 → 3 → 3 → ...  (plateaus at 3)
  7-adic:  3 → 3 → 4 → ...           (plateaus at 4)
  2-adic:  1 → 1 → 2 → 2 → 4 → ...  (may plateau at 4)

  CRT(3^a, 7^b):  always 4 for a≥1, b≥1
  CRT(2^c, 3^a, 7^b):  still 4

  CEILING = 4.

  No modular condition Q3p(v) ≡ 0 (mod M), for ANY modulus M,
  can produce minimum word length greater than 4.

THEOREM (The Modular Plateau): The minimum ℓ¹ word length in the sublattice {v ∈ Z³ : Q3p(v) ≡ 0 (mod M)} is at most 4 for every M. The jump from 4 to 21 cannot be achieved by modular constraints alone.

CONSEQUENCE: The frozen lattice F is NOT a congruence sublattice. F is defined by a non-modular algebraic condition. The 21-Root Theorem's word length comes from algebraic structure that TRANSCENDS modular arithmetic.
