Panel 1 — Question

What is the second Jacobi rotation's input matrix?

After H139's first rotation diagonalized the brace-box block, the remaining structure is the Schur complement of Q00 — the 2×2 block that remains after the brace degree of freedom is eliminated. This instrument computes that block exactly, finds its eigenvalues, and discovers the 42-digit gap that explains why the second rotation is unnecessary.

Method: The Schur complement of Q00 in Q is S = [[Q11,Q12],[Q12,Q22]] − (1/Q00)·[[Q01],[Q02]]·[Q01,Q02]. We compute S_scaled = Q00·S to stay in exact BigInt arithmetic.
