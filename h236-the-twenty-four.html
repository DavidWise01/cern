H236: The Three 24s Are Different Objects

24 appears in three contexts — Cl(3,0) grade spectrum (3×8), box content (4×6), and Cl(1,2) real product count (2×4×3) — but these are three different mathematical objects sharing a value. The H235 derivation is structural: the C/C parity split gives 2×C×(C−1)=24 real and 2×C²=32 imaginary products. The convergence on 24 is explained by 24 = 2³×3 being 3-smooth and highly composite: any {2,3}-generated system will produce 24 frequently. This is number-base bias, not deep structure. 🧒 Toddler Version

The number 24 keeps showing up. Is it magic?

NO. Here's why: 24 = 8 × 3 = 4 × 6 = 2 × 12. It has LOTS of ways to split it into pieces. And ALL those pieces happen to be our special numbers (8=OCHO, 3=bodies, 4=escape gap, 6=entries).

That's because 24 is built from ONLY 2 and 3: 24 = 2³ × 3. Our whole system is built from 2 and 3. So of COURSE 24 keeps appearing — any number made from just 2s and 3s will show up a lot in a system that runs on 2s and 3s!

The three 24s are three DIFFERENT things that happen to equal the same number. Like how "two dozen" and "hours in a day" are both 24, but for completely different reasons.

Known-Bad Control (KB-1)

The following claim is FALSE and must be detected as dead:

The three appearances of 24 are the SAME mathematical object seen from three angles, proving a deep structural unity. 24 appears because it is the unique number that equals both C×entries AND bodies×OCHO AND 2×C×(C−1), and no other number satisfies all three. The convergence cannot be explained by 24 being 3-smooth — 24 contains the prime factor 5.

The three 24s are DIFFERENT objects. Many numbers satisfy multiple factorizations (e.g., 12=2×6=3×4=4×3). The convergence IS explained by 3-smoothness. 24=2³×3 has no factor of 5.
