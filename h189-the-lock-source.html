🧒 Toddler Version

Imagine you have a jar of marbles. You can count them TWO ways:

Way 1: Line them up and count the gaps → you get 17.

Way 2: Sort them by color and count the groups → you also get 17.

These are TOTALLY different ways of counting. Lining up has nothing to do with colors. But somehow, for OUR jar, both ways give 17.

We checked: there's no RULE that says they HAVE to match. For most jars, they give different numbers. Our jar is special because its gaps and its colors happen to agree.

We know WHERE the 17 comes from (we traced both paths). We DON'T know WHY they agree. That's the last mystery.
